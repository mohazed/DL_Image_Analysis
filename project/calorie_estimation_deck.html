01 · The problem

Calories aren't in the pixels.

  • Single photo → one continuous kcal value. Regression, scored by MAE — which is minimised by the conditional median, not the mean.
  • Two dishes can share a silhouette yet differ by hundreds of kcal. The model must infer ingredient density × portion, not just "what food is this".
  • Test is 30% public / 70% private. The public leaderboard is a decoy — the honest signal is cross-validation, and that framing drives the whole pipeline.

05 · The fix that makes every later number trustworthy

Group-aware, source-stratified CV.

  • Build groups per source — perceptual-hash near-duplicates (Hamming ≤ 4) unioned with exact-calorie clusters, via union-find.
  • StratifiedGroupKFold on source × calorie-quintile — no group ever crosses a fold; every fold sees both sources across the full range.
  • Splits frozen to disk so every experiment is comparable.

07 · Input pipeline

Letterbox, don't crop.

  • Aspect-preserving resize to 384² + pad, for both sources. A crop cuts off food on a big plate; a squash distorts apparent portion — the exact thing we're measuring.
  • EXIF-transpose fixes Source-B phone rotation (portrait & landscape raws) before anything else.
  • Decode once → stacked .npy cache → copied to local SSD. B's 12 MP JPEGs are far too expensive to re-decode every epoch.

08 · Augmentation

Augment the view — never the portion.

If an augmentation changes how much food appears to be on the plate, it corrupts the label. So the transforms are source-specific and deliberately conservative.

10 · The loss — the sharpest decision

Predict in log space, penalise in kcal space.

loss = |expm1(z) − y_kcal|
# z ≈ log1p(kcal) from the net
  • The net outputs log1p(kcal) to tame the skew — but the loss inverts the transform before comparing.
  • So gradients scale with predicted kcal → Source B's large errors are automatically up-weighted, matching what MAE rewards.

Why not plain L1 on log1p?

Log-space L1 treats a proportional error equally — a 30-kcal miss on a salad = a 900-kcal miss on a feast. That down-weights B, exactly backwards.
Huber(δ≈100 kcal) kept as a stability fallback · gradient-norm clip at 1.0.

11 · Training & reproducibility

One config, two GPUs, bit-for-bit re-runs.

Runs anywhere, same trajectory

  • GPU-adaptive micro-batch + gradient accumulation → effective batch fixed at 32. An A100 and a T4 follow the same optimisation path.
  • bf16 on A100 · fp16 + GradScaler on T4/L4.
  • Resumable best + last checkpoints — a Colab disconnect costs one epoch.

Reproducible by construction

  • Full seeding · deterministic cuDNN · seeded dataloaders.
  • Offline pretrained weights, pinned versions.
  • A verification cell recomputes every EDA stat from disk and fails loudly on any mismatch.
  • SMOKE_TEST exercises the whole path in minutes.

12 · Calibration & inference

Ensemble, calibrate, clip.

  • Per-source calibration fit on OOF only, cross-validated across identity / shift / affine — kept only if it beats identity by > 1 fold-std.
  • 5 fold-best checkpoints × TTA — full 8-way dihedral for A, h-flip for B — averaged in kcal space.
  • Per-source clip to [train_min, p99.5]; exact-format submission guarded by assertions.

14 · What to remember

Three things carried the result.

01 · METHOD

EDA-driven. Two sources, a heavy tail, and a portion signal — every architectural choice is an answer to a measured fact, not a default.

02 · VALIDATION

Leakage-aware CV is the real deliverable. With 95.7% duplicate labels, group-aware folds are what make any number believable.

03 · OBJECTIVE

Match the loss to the metric. Penalising in kcal space up-weights Source B — where MAE is actually won or lost.
